Dot Matrix: three chart and skin directions

Same live data, three different ideas of what a dot-matrix chart is. Each has a light and a dark switch (monochrome both ways) and the six-page navigation proposed for this version. Open one to see it at full size.

A. LED boardValues are lit dots on a grid; the unlit grid stays visible. Doto lettering, black screen.
B. Dither printTone is the density of one-bit dots and stripes; lines become edges. Silkscreen lettering, white sheets.
C. Receipt tapeCharts are typeset: braille lines, block bars, shade-character calendar. Courier Prime on a grey tape.
